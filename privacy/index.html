Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Summary: Hoursheet collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your sheets stay on your iPhone, and files you export go only where you send them.

1. Who we are

Hoursheet is an iPhone app provided by MacLarens Studio (“we”).

2. Data we do not collect

We don't collect, receive or store any personal data through the app. Hoursheet has no sign-in and no account, we run no server for it, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs. The app doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't use the advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission.

3. Your sheets

Everything you enter (sheets, rows, labels, rates and presets) is stored on your iPhone only, in the app's own storage. A few settings, such as your default currency and rounding, are kept in the app's preferences on the device. We can't see, read or recover any of it.

Like other app data, your sheets are included in your iPhone backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to your computer. Those backups belong to your Apple Account or your computer, and we have no access to them. Hoursheet doesn't sync your sheets between devices.

4. Exports

When you export a sheet as text, CSV or PDF, the file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you send it with the share sheet (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or another app). That transfer is handled by Apple or by the app you choose, not by us.

5. Purchases

The optional Lifetime Unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple Account. Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Ratings

Now and then the app may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Whether and how you rate is handled entirely by Apple; the app only counts, on your iPhone, how often you have used it.

7. Emails to support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, including the app version, iOS version and device that the in-app support email adds. We use this only to answer you, don't share it, and delete it when it is no longer needed for your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete single sheets, or every sheet with Settings › Delete All Data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request, apart from emails you have sent us.

9. Children

Hoursheet is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you and to complain to a data protection authority. Since the app collects no data, these rights concern only emails you have sent us; write to ssx51f@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts to collect data, the policy will say so before that version is released.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, write to ssx51f@gmail.com.
