Frequently asked questions

How do I add rows to a sheet?

Open a sheet on the Sheets tab and tap +. A work day has a start, an end and a break, and Hoursheet works out the duration (an end before the start counts across midnight). A duration is just a time with a label, for example “Travel 1:30”. Tap any row to edit it in place. Week Template adds seven work days at once.

How are totals, rounding and pay worked out?

The total is shown as hours and minutes (h:mm) and as decimal hours. If you set an hourly rate on the sheet, Hoursheet shows the pay as well, in the sheet's currency. Rounding (to the nearest minute, quarter hour or 6 minutes) is applied to each row; the total is the sum of the rounded rows, and the pay is worked out once from that total.

What is free and what does the unlock add?

Free, for good: one sheet with full totals, rate, rounding and pay, the time difference and dates screens, and export as text and CSV. The one-time Lifetime Unlock adds unlimited saved sheets, PDF export and presets for rates and rounding. There is no subscription and no ads. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought the unlock with, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the unlock screen. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so it works on every iPhone you use with it.

How do I export a sheet?

Open the sheet and tap Export: choose Text, CSV or PDF and send the file with the share sheet (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or another app). CSV opens in Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets. The exported files are in English.

Are my sheets synced or backed up?

Your sheets are stored only on your iPhone. Hoursheet doesn't sync between devices and has no server. Your sheets are part of your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), so they move to a new iPhone when you set it up from a backup. To keep a copy elsewhere, export a sheet as CSV or PDF.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a sheet on the Sheets tab to delete it, or use Settings › Delete All Data to remove every sheet at once. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. We hold no copy, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Hoursheet runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later, in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Japanese and Korean. It works fully offline.
